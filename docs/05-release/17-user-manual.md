# 17. User Manual & Reception Desk Operations Guide

> **Document ID:** DOC-OPS-017  
> **Audience:** Front-Desk Receptionists & Operations Staff  
> **Language:** English with Arabic Operational Guidance (دليل الاستخدام لموظف الاستقبال)

---

## 1. Daily Opening Checklist (إجراءات فتح الوردية اليومية)

1. Turn on the reception PC and ensure the USB QR scanner is plugged into an active USB port.
2. Launch **Forge Fitness** from the desktop shortcut.
3. Enter your assigned username and password on the Login screen.
4. If working in low ambient lighting, click the Theme icon in the top-right corner to toggle **Dark Mode**.
5. Ensure the language is set to your preferred mode (**العربية** with native RTL or **English**).

---

## 2. Check-In & Entry Verification (تسجيل الحضور والدخول)

The **Attendance Page** is the default operating screen during open gym hours.

### 2.1 Fast Check-In via USB QR Scanner
1. Ask the incoming member to present their QR membership card or phone screen to the red scanner light.
2. The scanner automatically reads the card, transmits the ID, and presses enter.
3. **Inspect Visual & Audible Feedback:**
   - 🟢 **Green Banner + Pleasant Chime:** Access approved! The member's name, active sport discipline, and remaining sessions will display clearly.
   - 🔴 **Red Banner + Low Buzz:** Access denied! The reason will display:
     - *"Subscription Expired"* -> Guide the member to renew their plan.
     - *"Subscription Frozen"* -> Ask if they want to unfreeze today.
     - *"Already Checked in Today"* -> Informs that entry was already logged for today.

### 2.2 Manual Check-In (Forgot Card)
1. If a member forgot their card, click the **"Manual Check-In"** button (or type their phone number directly in the search bar).
2. Select the member from the list and click **"Confirm Entry"**.

---

## 3. Registering a New Member (تسجيل مشترك جديد)

1. Click **"Members"** on the navigation sidebar.
2. Click the **"+ New Member"** button in the top toolbar.
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Full Name (الاسم بالكامل):** e.g., "أحمد محمد علي"
   - **Phone Number (رقم الهاتف):** Must be 11 digits and unique in the system.
   - **Gender (النوع):** Male or Female.
   - **Medical Notes (ملاحظات صحية):** e.g., "علاج طبيعي للركبة" or "ربو خفيف".
4. Click **"Save Member"**.
5. Once saved, click **"Print Card"** to generate their official physical QR access card.

---

## 4. Selling & Renewing Subscriptions (تجديد وبيع الاشتراكات)

1. Locate the member in the directory and click **"New Subscription"**.
2. **Choose Plan Type:**
   - **Time-Based (محدد بالمدة):** For regular gym access. Select duration (1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year).
   - **Session-Based (محدد بالجلسات):** For martial arts, boxing, or private training. Enter session pool (e.g. 12 sessions).
3. If the gym manager authorized a seasonal discount, enter the discount percentage (e.g., `10%`).
4. Collect the cash payment from the member.
5. Click **"Confirm & Activate Plan"**.
6. The plan is now active immediately and ready for entry validation.
